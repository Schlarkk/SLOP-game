Simple.. A fishing game with a hint of cooking and farming.

The core mechanic

FISHING
You hold out your rod in a pod, after a while the bobber bobs and a fish is there.
You press space to reel it in.
then you need to SPAM certain buttons to reel it in, once you did that it comes out with a rarity and puts your combo meter to 1.
this way you have the dopamine of a combo, and the dopamine of it being RARE

the rarer a fish the more combo meter it gives.
